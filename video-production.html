ShoutIn&Out produces video for Mumbai-based SMEs, D2C brands, and B2B companies that need a full shoot — concept, crew, and equipment — not just a camera pointed at a product, built to work across ads, social content, and brand films.

The problem most Mumbai video production doesn't actually solve

Most production pitches sell equipment and crew credentials — a cinema camera, a lighting kit, a team of specialists — without connecting any of it to what the footage is actually for. A gorgeously shot video with no plan for where it's published, or how it's cut down for different platforms, is expensive footage sitting unused after the shoot day. The real problem is planning production around distribution, not just capture.

That means knowing before the shoot which cuts you need — a 30-second ad, a 15-second reel, a 2-minute brand film — so one shoot day produces months of usable content instead of one polished video.

How we work

  1. Concept & plan — defining what the footage needs to become (ads, reels, a brand film) before the shoot is scheduled.
  2. Shoot — full-service production with crew, equipment, and direction on the day.
  3. Edit & deliver — footage cut into every format planned for, not just one master video.
  4. Distribute-ready handoff — files delivered sized and formatted for wherever they're publishing.

Why this matters more than it did two years ago

A single shoot day is expensive to repeat, so the value of production work is decided before the camera ever rolls — in how many usable pieces of content one day can realistically produce. Planning distribution first is what turns one shoot into a content library instead of one video.

What does this actually cost, honestly?

Video production pricing in India in 2026 varies widely by scope: a single-location social content shoot often runs roughly ₹15,000–₹50,000 per day, while a full brand film or ad campaign production with crew, equipment, and multi-day shoots is quoted per project based on scale.
